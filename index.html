Our Story & Heritage

Our Story

Crafting moments, one bean at a time.

Born from an enduring reverence for the simple morning cup, Flavored began as an intimate boutique roastery dedicated to the purity of coffee craftsmanship. We traverse high-altitude volcanic soils across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Guatemala to partner directly with generational farmers who preserve ancestral agronomy.

Each small batch is gently flame-roasted to coax out the origin’s delicate terroir—never over-roasted, never rushed. In every porcelain vessel, we honor both the hands that harvested the red coffee cherry and the spirit of mindful pauses in a hurried world.
